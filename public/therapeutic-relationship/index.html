What is The Therapeutic Relationship?

Healing thrives with a Human-Therapist

Only Human-Therapists create real healing in mental health care, because only Human-Therapists offer the Therapeutic Relationship.

Therapy uses the Therapeutic Relationship. The Therapeutic Relationship is between two human people. Without that human interaction, Therapy does not exist.

It is well known in our industry

The Therapeutic Relationship is the primary factor for a client’s healing. Silicon Valley doesn’t want the public to know this well-researched fact.

“Studies have shown that the quality of the therapeutic relationship often predicts outcomes better than the specific techniques used.” Carrie Steckl, Ph.D., “The Importance of the Therapeutic Relationship,” mentalhealth.com. Editor: Rebecca Rydell. Reviewer: Carlos Protzel, Psy.D.

No one is always supposed to like their therapist

We react to different human beings differently. How we emotionally react and behave with a Human-Therapist gives us an opportunity to learn about ourselves.

The Therapeutic Relationship exists to recreate the interactions and relationships we have outside of Therapy, with other real people. In a contained environment, with a real human trained in psychotherapy, we can understand our own thoughts, emotions and behavior.

Sometimes you are supposed to be upset, angry, confused or disappointed by your therapist, so you can learn how to repair, how to hold yourself accountable, how to hold your Human-Therapist accountable, how to apologize, or how to accept care. Those lessons carry into the other relationships in your life.

Humans are not expected to interact like happy robots. Human-Therapists are not supposed to be perfect humans. The Therapeutic Relationship is a human relationship meant to guide you toward new understandings about yourself.

What is a Human-Therapist?

A Human-Therapist believes in and uses the Therapeutic Relationship, no matter what Therapy modality they specialize in.

  • A Human-Therapist takes a stance against tech companies re-defining Therapy without the Therapeutic Relationship.
  • A Human-Therapist will not work for subscription-based “Therapy platforms” as a mental-health gig worker.
  • A Human-Therapist supports a client’s well-being.
“As new generations have evolved to state that they strive to be Feminist, or Anti-racist, or Inclusive of all genders, therapists have entered an era where we must also state we are, Human-Therapists.”

For providers: There is no fee and no monetary expectations. Human-Therapist.org is a place to hold an idea and to bring therapist associations together, as long as we continue to support the Therapeutic Relationship.

For the public: When people understand the value of the Therapeutic Relationship with a Human-Therapist, tech companies will have difficulty taking over the industry.
